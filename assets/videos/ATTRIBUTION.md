# workout30.mp4

The team's own 33-minute standing cardio workout video (published by the team
on YouTube, https://youtu.be/WlTpamB8mkI). 360p, video only (no audio track).
Used as the trainer video of the "Follow the video: 30 min cardio" routine;
`assets/reference/workout30.timeline.json` was made from it with
`tool/pose_model/video_to_timeline.py --hz 5`.
